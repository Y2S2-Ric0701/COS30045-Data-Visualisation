OUR APPROACH

Clarity for a
brighter home.

Powerwise is a student-led data visualisation project making appliance energy information easier to understand for Australian households.

WHY IT MATTERS

Energy data should be useful, not overwhelming.

Appliance labels contain valuable information, but tables of technical specifications can be difficult to compare. We bring key measures such as annual energy consumption, screen size and star ratings together so you can spot the trade-offs at a glance.

Our television examples draw on the Australian government registration dataset. Values shown are indicative and intended for learning and comparison, not a substitute for the label on a specific product.
